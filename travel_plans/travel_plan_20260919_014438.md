## 🎟️ How to Save on Attractions  

| Attraction | Savings Trick |
|------------|---------------|
| **Eiffel Tower** | Book **2nd‑floor** only (cheaper than summit) and purchase online to avoid queue fees. |
| **Louvre / Orsay / Musée d’Orsay / Centre Pompidou** | Purchase a **Paris Museum Pass (3‑day)** – €52 ≈ ₹4,300 – covers 60+ museums, saves ~₹3,500‑₹5,000. |
| **Versailles** | Buy the **Passport** ticket online (skip‑the‑line) and combine with a **RER** pass. |
| **Seine Cruise** | Use **Bateaux-Mouches** night ticket (often €14) – cheaper than daytime combos. |
| **Public Transport** | 5‑day “Paris Visite” pass (zones 1‑2) – €38 ≈ ₹3,200; cheaper than buying single tickets for every ride. |
| **Dining** | Eat “prix‑fixe” lunch menus (≈ €12‑15) at cafés; they’re cheaper than dinner menus and still delicious. |
| **Souvenirs** | Shop at **Marché aux Puces de Saint‑Ouen** (flea market) for authentic items at lower prices than tourist shops. |
